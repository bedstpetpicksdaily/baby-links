TOG Ratings Explained: What You Actually Need for Fall

TOG stands for "Thermal Overall Grade" — essentially a measure of how much warmth a fabric traps. Lower TOG means lighter and cooler; higher TOG means cozier. For most nurseries, the sweet spot looks like this: 0.5 TOG for rooms 74–78°F (great for early fall and warm climates), 1.0 TOG for 69–73°F (the classic autumn workhorse), and 2.5 TOG for 61–68°F (deep fall into winter). Pair the TOG with the right layer underneath — a short-sleeve bamboo bodysuit under a 1.0 TOG, or a long-sleeve bamboo pajama set under a 2.5 TOG — and you've got a repeatable formula instead of nightly guesswork.

Why Bamboo Beats Cotton and Fleece for Cool Nights

Here's the part TOG numbers don't tell you: fabric behavior matters as much as thickness. Fleece traps heat but doesn't breathe, so babies who run warm can overheat even at a modest TOG. Cotton breathes well but holds moisture, leaving damp pajamas against skin as the room cools overnight. Bamboo viscose does both jobs — it's breathable and thermoregulating, wicking moisture away while still insulating. It's also hypoallergenic and exceptionally gentle, which makes it a favorite for babies with eczema or reactive skin. For fall, when your nursery might swing eight degrees between bedtime and dawn, that adaptability is the whole ballgame.

How to Build a Fall Sleep Wardrobe That Actually Works

Start with two sleep sacks in your primary TOG so one can be in the wash — most families in a 68–72°F home do best with a 1.0 TOG rotation. Frequently Asked Questions

What TOG sleep sack is best for fall?

For most nurseries kept between 69–73°F, a 1.0 TOG bamboo sleep sack is ideal. Drop to 0.5 TOG for warmer early-fall nights above 74°F, and move to 2.5 TOG once the room dips into the low 60s.

Is bamboo better than cotton for baby sleep sacks?

Bamboo viscose is more breathable and moisture-wicking than cotton, and it regulates temperature better across a swinging overnight range. It's also softer and naturally hypoallergenic, which helps babies with sensitive or eczema-prone skin.

When should my baby switch from a swaddle to a sleep sack?

Stop swaddling as soon as your baby shows any signs of rolling — typically around 8 to 12 weeks. A sleeveless bamboo sleep sack is the safe next step, giving arms freedom to move while keeping baby warm without loose blankets.
