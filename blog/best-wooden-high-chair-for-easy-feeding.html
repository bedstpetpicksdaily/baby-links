What to Look For

  • Removable, dishwasher-safe tray: The single biggest game-changer for easy feeding is a tray you can actually remove and throw in the dishwasher. Look for trays with minimal crevices and a smooth surface that won't trap pureed food or formula.
  • Open frame design with minimal fabric: Wooden high chairs with minimal padding or fabric around the seat are dramatically easier to wipe down than mesh or cloth models. Food inevitably falls into crevices, so a simple wood frame with a wipeable seat is your best bet for quick cleanup.
  • Stable, splayed legs with a footrest: A solid wooden base with wide legs prevents tipping when your growing baby gets wiggly. A footrest isn't just comfort—it gives your child something to push against when they're getting frustrated, which actually reduces flailing and spilled meals.
