What to Look For

  • Easy-to-Remove, Dishwasher-Safe Trays: Look for trays that detach with one or two simple motions and fit in your dishwasher. You'll be cleaning this after every meal, sometimes multiple times a day, so convenience here directly impacts your willingness to use the high chair regularly. Trays that require complex unlatching or hand-washing only create frustration when you're managing a hungry baby.
  • Raised Edges and Deep Wells: A tray with slightly raised edges and compartmented sections actually contains food and liquids rather than letting them slide directly into your baby's lap or onto the floor. Deep cup holders prevent sippy cups from tipping, and textured surfaces help dishes stay put during the inevitable squirming that happens during meals.
  • Wide, Stable Base with Adequate Legroom: The tray should sit far enough from your baby's body that you can comfortably sit across from them during feeding, and the overall chair base should feel genuinely stable when your growing toddler inevitably rocks or wiggles. A wobbly high chair adds unnecessary anxiety to an already hands-full situation.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What size tray should a high chair have for easy feeding?

    Look for trays that are at least 14-16 inches wide to provide enough surface area for plates, bowls, and utensils without food constantly falling off. A removable tray is ideal because it makes cleaning much easier and allows you to pull the high chair closer to your dining table for family meals.

    Are removable high chair trays worth it?

    Yes, removable trays are worth the investment because they save significant cleanup time and can be thrown directly in the dishwasher. They also make feeding more convenient since you can detach the tray when your child isn't eating or when transitioning to sitting at the regular table.

    How do I choose a high chair tray that's easy to clean?

    Choose a tray made from smooth, non-porous materials like polypropylene or silicone rather than textured plastic that traps food particles. Ensure the tray has no crevices or gaps where water and food can accumulate, and verify it's dishwasher-safe for convenient cleaning.

    What is the best high chair tray for a messy eater?

    Deep or raised-edge trays are best for messy eaters because they catch spills and prevent food from easily sliding onto the floor. Look for trays with a slightly concave surface and at least a 1-inch rim, and consider high chairs with removable trays that can be quickly swapped out when accidents happen.
