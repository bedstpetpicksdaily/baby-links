Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best personalized baby gift for a baby shower?

A personalized name blanket is the safest and most-loved choice, because it's used immediately for hospital and newborn photos. If the name isn't public yet, a monogrammed nursery storage basket or last-name keepsake works just as well.

How far in advance should I order personalized baby gifts?

Plan for two to three weeks. Customized items are made to order, and embroidery or printing adds production time on top of shipping. During holiday season, order four weeks out to be safe.

Are personalized baby gifts returnable if the name is misspelled?

Custom items are generally final sale, since they can't be resold. That said, if the error was the retailer's, most brands including MamaRaya will work with you — always screenshot your order confirmation showing the spelling you submitted.
