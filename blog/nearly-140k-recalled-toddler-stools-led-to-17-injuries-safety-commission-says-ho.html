Nearly 140K Toddler Stools Recalled: What Parents Need to Know Right Now

A major recall affecting nearly 140,000 toddler stools has resulted in 17 reported injuries, according to the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission. If you've purchased a toddler step stool recently, it's important to know whether your product is affected and what steps you should take immediately.

Why This Matters

Toddler stools are everyday items in many households—used to help little ones reach sinks, counters, and toilets independently. When products this common have safety issues, it directly impacts the daily routines and well-being of thousands of families. The reported injuries from this recall underscore the importance of staying informed about product safety alerts and acting quickly when they affect items in your home.

What Parents And Caregivers Should Know

  • Check the manufacturer, model number, and batch dates of any step stools you own against official CPSC recall notices
  • Stop using the recalled product immediately and keep it away from children
  • Contact the manufacturer for a refund, replacement, or repair—most will process claims quickly once notified
  • Sign up for CPSC recall alerts at SaferProducts.gov to be notified of future safety issues affecting items in your home
  • Keep receipts and product documentation for easier recall claim processing
