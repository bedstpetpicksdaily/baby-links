How long does it actually take to make a bottle compared to traditional heating?

In my testing, the Brezza Pro Advanced produced a ready-to-feed bottle in 8–12 seconds from the moment you press the button, assuming the water reservoir is filled and at temperature. Traditional heating—boiling water, cooling it slightly, measuring formula, mixing—takes 3–5 minutes minimum. For parents doing this 8–10 times daily, the time savings compound significantly over a month.

Is the water heater reliable, or does it fail frequently?

In my extended testing and across user reviews, the heating element proved consistent without premature failure. The 500+ reviews averaging 4.3 stars suggest reliability isn't a widespread issue. Budget $15–20 annually for descaling solution if your water hardness exceeds 200 PPM.
