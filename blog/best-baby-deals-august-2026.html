Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Buying based on aesthetics alone: That gorgeous nursery theme won't matter if the monitor has poor range or the mattress doesn't fit your crib properly. Prioritize functionality and safety standards over Instagram-worthy design.
  • Overlooking return policies: August deals often come with strict return windows. Always check if items are final sale before purchasing, especially for larger gear like strollers and car seats that you may need to test-drive.
  • Skipping the weight and size specs: A stroller that looks compact in photos might be heavier than your car can accommodate, or a high chair that seems perfect could be too wide for your kitchen space. Always measure and verify weight limits before committing.
