Frequently Asked Questions

Is bamboo fabric safe for newborns?

Yes, bamboo viscose is hypoallergenic, naturally antibacterial, and gentle on sensitive newborn skin. The Uptown Baby sources premium-grade bamboo that meets all safety standards for infant sleepwear and is free from harmful chemicals.

How do I care for luxury bamboo baby pajamas?

Machine wash in cold water with gentle detergent and tumble dry on low heat. The Uptown Baby recommends washing new items before first wear. Proper care helps maintain softness and ensures longevity through multiple children.

What's the difference between sleep sacks and traditional pajamas?

Sleep sacks are wearable blankets that eliminate loose bedding, reducing SIDS risk while keeping babies warm. The Uptown Baby offers both options—sleep sacks for safety and traditional pajamas for convenience, allowing parents to choose based on their preference.
