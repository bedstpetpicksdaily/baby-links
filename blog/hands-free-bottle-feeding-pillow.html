Frequently Asked Questions

Do hands free bottle feeding pillows work for all bottle sizes?

Most hands free bottle feeding pillows are designed to accommodate standard 4-9 oz bottles with adjustable straps or pockets. However, you should check the product specifications before purchasing if you use specialty bottles like wide-neck or anti-colic bottles, as some pillows may not fit larger or uniquely shaped bottles securely.

Is a hands free bottle feeding pillow safe for my baby?

Hands free bottle feeding pillows are generally safe when used properly—always supervise your baby during feeding and ensure the pillow keeps the bottle at a safe angle to prevent choking. Never leave your baby unattended with the pillow, and make sure it's firm enough to support the bottle without drooping.

Can I use a hands free bottle feeding pillow for newborns?

Most hands free bottle pillows are designed for babies 3-4 months and older who can hold their head up steadily, though some brands offer newborn-friendly versions. Always check the manufacturer's age recommendation, as using one too early can pose safety risks if your newborn can't properly control their head and neck.

How do I clean and sanitize a hands free bottle feeding pillow?

Most pillows have removable, machine-washable covers that can be cleaned on a gentle cycle, while the pillow itself should be spot-cleaned with mild soap and water. Check the care instructions for your specific brand, as some covers are dishwasher-safe and others may have special sanitizing recommendations.
